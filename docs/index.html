Predicting Clinical Limb Apraxia Scores from Upper-Limb Pantomime Kinematics

Daanish M. Mulla1, Aaron L. Wong2,3, Laurel J. Buxbaum2,3, & J. Andrew Michaels1

1Centre for Vision Research, York University, Toronto, ON, Canada · 2Jefferson Moss Rehabilitation Research Institute & 3Department of Rehabilitation Medicine, Thomas Jefferson University, Philadelphia, PA, USA

Across 53 participants (10 healthy controls, 26 left-hemisphere stroke patients without apraxia, and 17 stroke patients with apraxia; 1,950 valid trials across 39 everyday tools), a two-pathway L2-regularized Ridge model blending participant-level movement profiles with reliability-weighted item-level predictions reproduces expert praxis ratings (r = 0.870, stroke AUC = 0.946 across all 53 participants; r = 0.776, AUC = 0.889 on 12 held-out test participants). Use the live browser model below to toggle kinematic features, test shorter clinical tool batteries, adjust pathway weights, and inspect individual patient decompositions in real time.
